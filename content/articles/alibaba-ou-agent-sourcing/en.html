Order directly on Alibaba, or go through a sourcing agent? We are a sourcing agent, so let us say it straight away: both options can be right. It depends on the product, the amount at stake and the time you have. Here is an honest comparison.

Ordering on Alibaba yourself: the advantages

  • Cost. You pay no intermediary. For a simple purchase, the saving is real.
  • Choice. Thousands of suppliers, visible prices, quick comparison. Within hours you have a feel for the market.
  • Platform protection. Alibaba.com offers an order protection programme (Trade Assurance) for payments made through the platform, under the conditions it sets. Some suppliers have also been verified by independent inspection bodies.
  • Learning. Negotiating yourself teaches you more about your product, your suppliers and your costs.

Ordering yourself: the limits

  • Factory or trading company? Many sellers are intermediaries. That is not necessarily a problem, but you need to know who you are dealing with.
  • The protection only applies on the platform. If the supplier asks for a direct transfer to another account, you usually lose it. It is also a warning sign.
  • Quality. A good sample does not guarantee the production run. Pre-shipment inspection is up to you.
  • Logistics to Luanda. Many prices are EXW or FOB: transport, insurance, consolidating several suppliers and customs clearance are left to you.
  • Time. Time difference, language, minimum order quantities, following production: it all takes hours.
  • Disputes. If something goes wrong, the supplier is more than 10,000 km away.

Using a sourcing agent: the advantages and the limits

A good agent checks the supplier before payment, negotiates, follows production, organises quality control and coordinates transport to the destination. With CHANCELENO you have one contact, one contract and one invoice, and a firm offer with price, lead time and Incoterm before you commit.

But an agent has a cost: fees, commission or a price that includes the service. And not all agents are transparent: some also take commissions from suppliers without telling the client. Always ask how the agent is paid. At CHANCELENO, the remuneration model is stated in every quote.

How to decide

Order yourself if the product is simple and standard, if the amount is one you can afford to lose without putting the business at risk, if you have the time and if you pay through the platform.

Use an agent for technical parts with precise part numbers, large amounts, several suppliers to consolidate, critical deadlines, or when you do not have time to follow the order.

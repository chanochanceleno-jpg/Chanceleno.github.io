Checking a part number: OE, chassis number, registration plate

The most common reason for a wrong part is a part number that was never properly identified. Before asking for a quote, gather:

  • the OE part number, meaning the truck manufacturer's number, stamped on the old part or shown on a previous invoice. Another manufacturer's number also helps, as long as the brand is given;
  • the chassis number (VIN), 17 characters long, on the manufacturer's plate and the registration document. It confirms the exact engine, gearbox and axle version;
  • the registration plate and year of the vehicle, which complete the identification, especially for trucks imported second-hand;
  • photos of the worn part, its label and the manufacturer's plate.

We then check the number against the manufacturer's catalogues and the suppliers' cross-reference tables. If anything is unclear, we ask you before ordering: it always costs less than a return shipment from Luanda.

Lead times and shipping from Europe and Asia to Luanda

The total lead time depends on part availability, the supplier and the mode of transport. As a guide:

  • Europe and Turkey: a part in stock sent by air usually reaches Luanda a few days after dispatch. By sea, allow three to five weeks in general, depending on port calls;
  • Asia (China, India, Thailand): by sea, generally five to eight weeks port to port. Air freight remains an option for urgent, light parts.

On top of this come order preparation and customs clearance at the port of Luanda, handled by your licensed customs broker (the "despachante oficial"). Every quote states the lead time, the Incoterm and the mode of transport. Parts from several suppliers travel in one shipment: lower costs and fewer documents.

Frequently asked questions

Do you supply genuine or aftermarket parts?

Both. Every quote names the manufacturer, the origin and the price and quality difference between the options. An aftermarket part is never presented as genuine.

Can I order a single part?

Yes, every request is reviewed, even for a single part number. If shipping makes a very small quantity uneconomical, we tell you so plainly.

I don't have the OE part number. Can you still help?

Yes. Send the chassis number, registration plate, year and photos of the part and of the manufacturer's plate: we identify the part number and confirm it with you before ordering.

How does payment work?

The quote can be issued in EUR, USD or AOA. You pay a deposit limited to what is needed to place the order, and the balance against the shipping documents.

Do you deliver outside Luanda?

Yes. We coordinate delivery to the agreed destination, in other provinces, in the DRC or in Congo, with partner freight forwarders.

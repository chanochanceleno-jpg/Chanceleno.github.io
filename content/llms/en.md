### The method

1. Your need: you send a part number, a list or a specification.
2. Firm offer: CHANCELENO sources and sends a firm offer (price, lead time, Incoterm).
3. Order: you confirm with a purchase order and pay a deposit, limited to the amount needed to launch the order.
4. Production: production is monitored (photos, videos, pre-shipment inspection).
5. Balance and delivery: the balance is paid at shipment, against the shipping documents and the inspection report; CHANCELENO coordinates delivery.
